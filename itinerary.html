Day by day

Ten days, roughly in this order.

DAY 01

Arrive in Bari, drive to Alberobello

Land in Bari, pick up the rental car, and head straight for the trulli. Settle in, walk the old town at dusk.

DAY 02

Alberobello

Full day in and around the trulli district. Slow morning, long lunch, no fixed plan.

DAY 03

Grotte di Castellana, then Ostuni

Morning cave tour, then drive to Ostuni and check into the masseria in time for sunset over the old town.

DAY 04–05

Ostuni and the coast

Base here for two days — beach days on the coast nearby, one evening in the old town for dinner.

DAY 06

Polignano a Mare

Drive up the coast. Swim at Lama Monachile, wander the cliffside streets, stay for dinner over the water.

DAY 07–09

Open — fill in as plans firm up

Placeholder days for Lecce, the Salento coast, or a slower pace — replace once decided.

DAY 10

Drive back to Bari, fly out

Return the car, buffer time for traffic, flight home.
