Reference

About the trip.

Dates

September 20–30. Ten days, flying into and out of Bari.

Getting around

Rental car for the whole trip — public transport between the smaller towns is thin. Roads in the trulli area are narrow; go slow through town centers.

Where we're staying

A masseria (working farm estate) near Ostuni for the middle stretch, with a night in Alberobello at the start. Full addresses and booking references live in the shared trip folder, not here.

Packing notes

  • Late September is warm but the water cools off — pack a light layer for evenings.
  • Old towns are cobblestone throughout; comfortable shoes over anything else.
  • Some churches and masserie expect shoulders covered.

Food to look for

Orecchiette, burrata (it's actually from this region), taralli, and the local olive oil — Puglia produces a huge share of Italy's.
